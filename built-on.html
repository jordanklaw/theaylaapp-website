What Ayla is built on

How the readings are made, what the Garden keeps, and the services underneath.

Narrates, never computes

Every chart position in Ayla is computed deterministically from real astronomical data, on our servers. A language model writes the prose of your reading, and it can only describe those positions, never change them. The same sky produces the same chart every time.

The daily reading

Five beats, every day. The Sky: what is actually overhead, in plain words. The Mirror: what that looks like bent toward your goal. The Frame: a shape you will recognize from something you already have open. The Move: one small action you choose. The Loop: the action becomes a seed in your Garden.

The Garden

The Garden is a ledger of seeds: the small things you said you would do, and what came of them. Weeks after you plant one, Ayla asks about it. There are no streaks; a quiet week reads as fallow, not failure.

Three modes

The Sun reads you. The World reads you and a friend. The Lovers reads you and a partner. For The World and The Lovers you add the other person's birth details; they never need to install anything.

What we use

Supabase hosts our database, authentication, and storage. Chart math uses a third-party chart service, called from our servers. Anthropic Claude generates narrative text server-side under zero-data-retention terms. RevenueCat and Apple handle subscriptions; we never see your payment details.

What Ayla will not do

Ayla never promises that the sky will deliver an outcome. Readings give context. What you do with a window is yours.

The stars do not decide. They open a window. You act in it.
